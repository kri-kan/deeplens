# Goal: Acquire 15,000 Pre-Registered VIP Customers via Gamified Multilingual Intake

## 1. Desired Result
Convert at least 12,500 to 15,000 qualified prospective buyers ($\approx 25\%\text{–}30\%$ of the existing 45k Instagram community) into authenticated, pre-registered VIP members within 60 days. Each profile captures verified E.164 WhatsApp numbers, preferred languages (English, Telugu, Hindi), zero-party styling and fabric preferences, and an earned Vayyari Mudras loyalty vault balance ahead of public storefront launch.

## 2. Why It Matters
Transitioning from passive social media followers and anonymous website scrollers (~80 visitors/day) to an identified, motivated customer base eliminates Day-1 launch risk. Pre-registered customers with earned shopping currency (Mudras) and stated wedding/festival purchase timelines convert at 5x to 8x the industry rate of cold storefront traffic.

## 3. Measurable Success Criteria & Metrics
- **Acquisition Volume:** 12,500 to 15,000 completed VIP intake surveys in 60 days ($\approx 200\text{–}250\text{ completions/day}$).
- **Completion Funnel Conversion:** $\ge 82\%$ completion rate among users initiating the survey.
- **Audience Qualification Depth:** $> 75\%$ of completed profiles identify explicit fabric and craft preferences (Kanjeevaram, Banarasi, Organza, Zari, Cutwork).
- **Viral Referral K-Factor:** $\ge 0.25$ (1 in 4 registered members successfully refers another prospective buyer).
- **Launch Day One-OTP Hydration:** $> 40\%$ of pre-registered cohort activates their account within the first 14 days of store opening.

## 4. Current Confidence & Understanding
- **Confidence:** `High`
- **Understanding:** High engagement observed on Instagram Reels and WhatsApp supplier groups. The bottleneck was lack of an engaging, gamified conversion bridge between social engagement and master customer records.
