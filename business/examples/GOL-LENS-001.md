# Goal: Sub-150ms Visual Saree Swatch Discovery for Mobile Shoppers

## 1. Desired Result
Enable mobile shoppers browsing ethnic wear in the Vayyari app to tap any fabric swatch or video still and receive visually identical saree catalog recommendations in under 150 milliseconds.

## 2. Why It Matters
Conversion rates on mobile ethnic wear drop by over 40% when visual similarity queries take longer than 500ms. Instant matching keeps high-intent luxury shoppers engaged.

## 3. Measurable Success Criteria & Metrics
- Search response p95 latency: Baseline 850ms $\to$ Target < 150ms.
- Visual swatch search engagement: > 25% of mobile shoppers utilize swatch search per session.
- Swatch-to-Cart conversion rate: Baseline 3.2% $\to$ Target 6.5%.

## 4. Current Confidence & Understanding
- Confidence: `High`
- Understanding: High correlation observed in preliminary WhatsApp pilot between instant image matches and buyer checkout velocity.
